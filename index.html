1400–1440Strong overallShows broad command of SAT material with meaningful room above it.Worth improving if highly selective schools are a major target.

Reading & Writing200–800 · performance meaning
780–800Essentially no systematic errorMisses are usually isolated judgment, wording, or time-pressure errors rather than a recurring skill gap.Do not rebuild the section; clean up rare mistakes.
740–770Very high masteryOnly a small number of question types are still producing repeatable misses.Audit errors by subtopic and eliminate the pattern.
700–730Strong, with identifiable leaksMost question types are reliable, but a few recurring skills or timing decisions still cost points.Target the weak clusters rather than reviewing everything.
650–690Competent but inconsistentYou understand much of the section, but systematic errors remain across several question families.Separate grammar, rhetoric, vocabulary, and evidence errors and drill them individually.
600–640Partial masteryRoutine questions are not yet consistently converted into points.Build dependable rules and reading habits before emphasizing the hardest items.
550–590Fundamentals are unstableMultiple core skills are producing repeated errors, not just occasional misses.Use subtopic practice heavily and review why every wrong answer failed.
500–540Frequent systematic errorsCore conventions, comprehension, or evidence selection are still unreliable.Focus on fundamentals and untimed accuracy before speed.
200–490Broad foundational gapsToo many basic question types are still unstable for hard-question practice to be efficient.Rebuild the section skill by skill.
Math200–800 · performance meaning
780–800Essentially no systematic errorMisses are usually isolated execution, interpretation, or time-pressure errors rather than a recurring concept gap.Polish rare mistakes; do not relearn the whole section.

700–730Strong, with identifiable leaksMost standard problems are secure, but several higher-level setups or concepts still recur as misses.Target those weak clusters instead of doing broad review.
650–690Competent but inconsistentCore algebra is mostly usable, but multiple domains still produce systematic errors.Separate concept gaps from algebraic execution and calculator mistakes.

500–540Frequent systematic errorsBasic setups and transformations are still unreliable across the section.Repair prerequisite skills before spending much time on hard questions.
