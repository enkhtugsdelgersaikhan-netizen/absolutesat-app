Text Structure and Purpose

Craft & Structure
What it tests

These questions ask why the author included a sentence, example, quotation, or comparison, or what the passage as a whole is designed to accomplish. You are analyzing the role a piece of text plays inside the larger argument. Common functions include introducing a problem, giving an example, qualifying a claim, presenting evidence, contrasting views, or explaining a mechanism.

How the SAT disguises it
  • The question focuses on one sentence but the answer depends on what comes before and after it.
  • A sentence gives an example; a distractor incorrectly calls it the main conclusion.
  • A passage shifts from an old view to new evidence.
  • The author's purpose is analytical rather than emotional, even if the topic is dramatic.
Example problem

For decades, biologists assumed that a certain desert moss remained dormant whenever rainfall was absent. Recent imaging, however, shows that the moss can briefly activate photosynthesis using moisture from overnight fog. This finding does not overturn the importance of rainfall, but it reveals that the moss has access to an additional water source. What is the function of the second sentence?

  • A) It presents evidence that complicates an earlier assumption.
  • B) It summarizes a debate about whether deserts receive rainfall.
  • C) It gives an example of a species that cannot photosynthesize.
  • D) It argues that rainfall is irrelevant to desert plants.
Answer: A
Deep-dive solution
  1. The first sentence gives the old assumption: no rainfall meant dormancy.
  2. The second introduces new imaging evidence showing activity during fog.
  3. The third sentence explicitly says the new evidence does not erase the importance of rainfall; it adds nuance.
  4. Therefore the second sentence's role is to complicate or revise the earlier assumption.
Fast method

Label each sentence's job in 2–4 words: old view, new evidence, qualification, conclusion. Function questions become much easier.

Common trap

Content and function are different. 'What does this sentence say?' is not the same as 'Why is this sentence here?'
