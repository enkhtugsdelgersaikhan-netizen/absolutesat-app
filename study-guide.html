RESEARCH BEHIND THE PLAN

College Board research associates full-length digital practice with higher SAT performance (2025). Earlier Official SAT Practice research identifies skill progression, full-length tests, and personalized skill recommendations as useful practice behaviors (2020). Both analyses are observational: they do not predict an individual score gain or prove that practice alone caused the reported differences.
